Capabilities

Signal Detection

Multi-source Robinhood Chain token scanning

●Uniswap V3/V4 — Top pools by volume, price data, liquidity depth on Robinhood Chain
●Blockscout — On-chain analytics, token safety, holder data, contract verification
●NOXA Fun — New token launches on the Robinhood Chain launchpad
●Helius DAS — On-chain safety checks (mint authority, freeze authority, honeypot detection)
●Runs every 5 minutes (configurable), processes 50+ tokens per cycle

Smart Scoring

6-factor weighted algorithm

●Volume Spike (25%) — Volume-to-liquidity ratio indicates trading interest
●Liquidity Depth (20%) — Minimum $10K liquidity required, deeper is better
●24h Momentum (20%) — Price change over 24 hours indicates trend direction
●1h Momentum (15%) — Short-term price action for early detection
●Holder Growth (10%) — Number of unique token holders
●Safety Score (10%) — Liquidity health and on-chain risk factors
Gates: 70%+ = Actionable 45%+ = Watchlist <45% = Noise

Auto Launch

AI-driven token deployment with human approval

●Condition Evaluation — Rule-based scoring analyzes signal strength, market heat, recent launches
●Confidence Score — Produces 0-100% confidence. Must hit 75%+ to propose a launch
●Approval Gate — You approve via API or Telegram before any token is deployed
●NOXA Fun — Deploy ERC-20 + locked Uniswap LP in one transaction, rug-proof from day one (~0.01 ETH)
●Uniswap V3 — Custom pool creation with full control over fee tiers and liquidity ranges
●Dedicated Wallet — Hot wallet funded by you, AI signs transactions

Guardian
